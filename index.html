Last Two Prime Ministers of India

1. Narendra Modi

Full Name: Narendra Damodardas Modi

Date of Birth: 17 September 1950

Political Party: Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP)

Prime Minister Tenure: 26 May 2014 – Present

Major Achievements

  • Digital India Initiative
  • Make in India Program
  • Swachh Bharat Abhiyan
  • Goods and Services Tax (GST)
  • Ayushman Bharat Scheme

2. Dr. Manmohan Singh

Full Name: Dr. Manmohan Singh

Date of Birth: 26 September 1932

Political Party: Indian National Congress (INC)

Prime Minister Tenure: 22 May 2004 – 26 May 2014

Major Achievements

  • Economic Reforms and Growth
  • Right to Information Act (RTI)
  • MGNREGA Employment Scheme
  • India-US Civil Nuclear Agreement
  • Expansion of Higher Education
